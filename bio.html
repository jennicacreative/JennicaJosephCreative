Hi I'm Jennica! I'm a graphic designer, former showgirl & part-time clown based in Las Vegas. I studied graphic design at College of Southern Nevada & have a particular interest in print work. You can see my work in ADDY Magazine 2025 where I was a designer for the magazine as well as having my work featured as an American Advertising Award winner. My creative journey brought me to Las Vegas to sing in the legendary show Jubilee! & now when I'm not designing you can find me on stage at Atomic Saloon Show where I understudy four characters.
